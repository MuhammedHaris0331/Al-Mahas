About Al Mahas

Al Mahas General Trading LLC is a UAE-based general trading company engaged in the distribution of premium FMCG products across the UAE market.

We specialize in beverages, juices, energy drinks, snacks, rice, spices, food products, and other fast-moving consumer goods sourced from trusted brands and suppliers.

At Al Mahas, we focus on delivering quality products, competitive pricing, reliable supply, and dependable service to retailers, supermarkets, wholesalers, and distribution partners across the region.
